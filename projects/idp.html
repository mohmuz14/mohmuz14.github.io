AI-Powered IDP System

Intelligent Document Processing

  • Azure AI
  • LLM
  • OCR
  • Python

Problem

Enterprise business documents arrive as unstructured PDFs and scans, so extracting and validating their contents is manual, slow and error-prone at volume.

Approach

Architected an Intelligent Document Processing platform combining OCR, LLM-based extraction, rule-based validation frameworks and analytics pipelines on Azure AI Foundry.

Results

Automates extraction, validation and processing of unstructured business documents end-to-end, taken from proof-of-concept through to production deployment.
