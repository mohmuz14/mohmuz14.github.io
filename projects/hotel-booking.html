Analyzing Hotel Booking

Booking Demand Analysis

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Data Visualisation

Problem

Hotels lose revenue to cancellations and misjudged demand, and the drivers are not obvious from booking records alone.

Approach

Analysed hotel booking demand data to profile guests, seasonality and cancellation behaviour, with exploratory analysis and visualisation of the strongest signals.

Results

Highlights the booking patterns and cancellation drivers that matter for demand planning.
