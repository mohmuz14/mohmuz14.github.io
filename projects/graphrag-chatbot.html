GraphRAG Enterprise Chatbot

GraphRAG + Neo4j Chatbot

  • GraphRAG
  • Neo4j
  • Azure
  • LLM

Problem

A Fortune 500 retail client needed answers that span related entities across enterprise knowledge, which flat vector retrieval alone could not resolve.

Approach

Built a GraphRAG chatbot integrating Neo4j knowledge graphs with structured retrieval and LLM-based reasoning, so queries traverse relationships rather than matching text alone.

Results

Enables deeper contextual search and multi-hop relationship discovery across enterprise knowledge for the client's teams.
