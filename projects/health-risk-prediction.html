Health Risks Prediction

Predictive Health Analytics

  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • Predictive Modeling

Problem

Health risks are easiest to act on early, but early indicators sit buried in routine tabular records.

Approach

Trained and evaluated classification models over health indicator data, with preprocessing, feature engineering and model comparison to select the strongest performer.

Results

Flags elevated-risk cases from routine indicators, supporting earlier intervention.
