Customer Churn Analysis

Churn Modelling and Retention

  • Python
  • Scikit-learn
  • Power BI

Problem

Retaining an existing customer costs far less than winning a new one, but churn signals are spread thinly across behavioural and account data.

Approach

Built an end-to-end churn pipeline covering data acquisition, preprocessing, model development and evaluation, with the drivers of churn surfaced for business review.

Results

Identifies customers at risk of leaving and the factors most associated with churn.
