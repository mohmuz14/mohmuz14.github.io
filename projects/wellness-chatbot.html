Wellness Chatbot with RAG

LLM Powered Wellness Chatbot

  • RAG
  • LLM
  • Streamlit
  • FAISS

Problem

Generic wellness advice ignores individual context, and users rarely stay engaged with static health guidance.

Approach

Built a retrieval-augmented chatbot over a curated wellness corpus using FAISS vector search and an LLM, with gamification layered in to sustain engagement, served through Streamlit.

Results

Returns personalised recommendations grounded in retrieved source material rather than unconstrained model output.
