Taiwan AQI Analysis

Air Quality Data Analysis

  • Python
  • Pandas
  • Data Visualisation

Problem

Air quality readings arrive as high-volume time-series across many monitoring stations, which is hard to interpret in raw form.

Approach

Cleaned and analysed Taiwan air quality index data across stations and time, then visualised spatial and seasonal patterns.

Results

Surfaces how air quality varies by station and season, turning raw sensor readings into readable trends.
